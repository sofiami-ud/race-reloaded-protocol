Points to settle

  • The paper does not fix where the evidence threshold sits. Here it comes after Q8, so an open boundary is recorded as “race not operative” even when evidence is thin.
  • The “Unclear” branch at Q8 is an addition of the coding grid: a boundary that cannot be judged sends the segment to “mode undetermined”.
  • Segments where race is not operative are kept, because extinction can only be shown by segments on the same theme in which race no longer works.
  • Mentioned-only segments receive a phenomenological record; whether they also count in the series is not stated in the paper.
